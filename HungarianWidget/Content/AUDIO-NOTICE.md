# Hungarian audio notes

The initial pronunciation clips are generated ahead of time with the Piper `hu_HU-anna-medium` Hungarian voice. They are bundled as local WAV files; the app does not need Piper, a speech voice installed in Windows, or an internet connection at runtime.

The voice is synthesized and may sound less natural than a human recording. The referenced voice card identifies its training dataset as CC0: <https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/v1.0.0/hu/hu_HU/anna/medium/MODEL_CARD>. Piper source: <https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl>.

Before public distribution, review the current license and attribution terms for both Piper and the voice model. The app itself does not bundle Piper or the model weights.
